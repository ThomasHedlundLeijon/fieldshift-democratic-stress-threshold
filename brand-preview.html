Section 07
Research Card Component
Fractal State Theory · Working Paper
State-Dependent Threshold Detection in Democratic Systems: A Fractal State Theory Approach
T. Hedlund-Leijon,  FieldShift Research Institute
Abstract
We propose a state-theoretic framework for identifying democratic fragility thresholds. Classical early warning models treat democratic decline as a continuous process; we demonstrate that the underlying dynamic is better characterized as a system occupying discrete states, with transitions concentrated at threshold crossings. Using V-Dem data (v16, 1900–2024, N=202 countries), we show that the FSI score discriminates pre-transition from stable states with AUC 0.87 at five-year forecast horizons. Crucially, predictive accuracy depends on correctly identifying the system's current state before applying the model — consistent with the central premise of Fractal State Theory.
